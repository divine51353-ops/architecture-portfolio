## About

I design and build AI workflows that people can check.

Most AI work breaks in the same places: a draft adds details nobody gave, a number drifts from its source, something is called "approved" before anyone approved it, and every new chat starts from zero. My work is the machinery around the model that catches those failures: facts traced to sources, approvals tied to the exact version someone reviewed, out-of-date work flagged automatically, and handoffs that software can verify before picking the work back up.

I build with Claude Code, deliver through Claude Skills and the Model Context Protocol, and write tests for every failure I fix. I label my work by what it has actually shown — architecture, prototype, tested release, or customer result — and I don't claim more.

Right now I'm finishing Voice DNA Early Access, the first SupremeOS product: an installable skill that builds a voice profile from writing you own, checks it with you through two short passages, and writes one draft in your voice from facts you confirm, with a reusable voice guide and handoff.

I work with consultants, founders, creators and small organizations who want AI that sounds like them and stays honest about what it knows.
