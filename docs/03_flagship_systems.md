# Flagship Systems

## 1. Narrative Continuity Engine

A long-form writing architecture designed to preserve story identity, continuity, baton movement, emotional key, and payoff integrity across chapters and sessions.

### Public value
- supports book systems and narrative studios
- reduces context collapse in long-form writing
- creates a repeatable story-governance workflow

## 2. CineRuntime Studio

A cinematic governance runtime for visual planning, continuity control, agent routing, validation, packaging, and franchise inheritance.

### Public value
- supports short-form, mid-form, and long-form cinematic planning
- structures agent/tool use through declared offices and fallback logic
- treats continuity and packaging as governed production concerns

## 3. Companion and operator systems

A family of onboarding, quick-reference, activation, and operator-facing systems used to make complex architectures usable by real people.

### Public value
- improves operator experience
- supports installs, onboarding, and delivery packs
- increases legibility for users and buyers

## 4. Expansion and licensing systems

A multiplication architecture for taking stable products and converting them into product families, installs, licensing pathways, and certification-ready delivery classes.

### Public value
- supports market expansion without losing architectural consistency
- formalizes commercialization beyond one-off services
