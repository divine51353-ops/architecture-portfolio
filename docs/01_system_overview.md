# System Overview

## Executive summary

This portfolio presents a governed AI architecture approach for building productized intelligence systems. The core design principle is simple: governance happens before output. Instead of treating AI as a generic generator, the system treats intelligence as something that must be routed, bounded, validated, and delivered through repeatable product surfaces.

## Core thesis

Most AI products focus on prompt quality or model quality. This portfolio focuses on:

- input classification
- workflow routing
- state carry
- continuity preservation
- validation before seal
- product-family consistency
- operator surfaces designed for deployment (see 12_evidence_and_maturity.md for what has actually run)

## Architecture layers

1. **Governance layer**  
   Category, policy, role, and boundary logic.

2. **Workflow layer**  
   Multi-agent execution patterns with specialist offices and explicit routing.

3. **State and retrieval layer**  
   Carried context, structured memory, and document-backed retrieval.

4. **Delivery layer**  
   HTML shells, ChatKit-style chat surfaces, and operator-first interfaces.

5. **Commercial layer**  
   Product ladder, delivery packs, installs, and licensing paths.

## What this approach is for

This architecture is best suited for domains where mistakes are expensive, context matters, and repeated output quality depends on controlled execution. Examples include:

- long-form writing and narrative systems
- nonprofit and compliance workflows
- brand and proposal systems
- cinematic planning and franchise continuity
- operator-facing AI tools

## Why this matters

The result is not a single assistant. It is a governed product family capable of supporting multiple vertical systems under one architectural standard.
