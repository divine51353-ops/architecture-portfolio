# Product Ecosystem

## Product-family model

The product ecosystem is structured as a governed family rather than isolated SKUs.

## Main public product lanes

- Book Forge Studio
- CineRuntime Studio
- Companion Runtime
- CIVITAS
- Brand / proposal / thought-leadership lanes
- installs and licensing paths

## Commercial ladder

- entry digital products
- workflow-backed products
- operator products
- studio installs
- licensing / enterprise

## Public/private line

The ecosystem is designed to let public products demonstrate value without exposing the full protected architecture behind them.
